# About: Random effects structure: Keep it maximal

**Type:** Article

**Link:** [Official resource](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jml.2012.11.001)

Barr et al. (2013), on random effects structure.

## Key details

- Barr et al. (2013) discuss maximal random-effects structures for confirmatory hypothesis testing.

## How to Use

1. Read the article's rationale and conditions for a maximal random-effects structure before specifying a confirmatory model. ([source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jml.2012.11.001))
2. Compare its recommendation with later evidence and your design's supported random-effects structure; do not treat the article as an automatic model recipe. ([source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jml.2012.11.001))
