# About: Improving Your Statistical Inferences

**Type:** Online book

**Link:** [Official resource](https://lakens.github.io/statistical_inferences/)

Online book by Daniel Lakens about improving statistical inferences.

## Key details

- Use the book as a topic-based reference for improving statistical inference.

## How to Use

1. Open the book contents and select the chapter that matches the inference issue in your analysis. ([source](https://lakens.github.io/statistical_inferences/))
2. Follow the chapter's examples and references, then record which assumptions and recommendations apply to your study. ([source](https://lakens.github.io/statistical_inferences/))
