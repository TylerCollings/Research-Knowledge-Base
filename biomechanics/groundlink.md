# About: GroundLink: A Dataset Unifying Human Body Movement and Ground Reaction Dynamics

**Type:** Dataset

**Link:** [Official resource](https://csr.bu.edu/groundlink/)

Dataset unifying human body movement and ground reaction dynamics.

## Key details

- The page provides subject-level C3D, FBX, SMPL/NPZ, and force-data downloads; raw force-plate data are also embedded in C3D files.

## How to Use

1. Choose a subject or the All Subjects row, then select the required C3D, FBX, SMPL/NPZ, or Force download for your analysis. ([source](https://csr.bu.edu/groundlink/))
2. Use the linked project paper and source code to understand the selected representation and preprocessing before combining it with other data. ([source](https://csr.bu.edu/groundlink/))
